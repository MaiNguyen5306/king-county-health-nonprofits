# Project Scope

## Working Title

Financial Resilience of King County Community Health Nonprofits

## Core Research Question

How do funding mix, program-spending allocation, and financial stability differ across community-health nonprofit service types and organization sizes?

## Geographic Scope

Organizations headquartered in King County, Washington.

An organization's filing address does not necessarily represent every community it serves.

## Time Period

Tax years 2019–2023.

## Organization Requirements

Organizations must:

- Be tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3)
- Be headquartered in King County
- Have filed the full Form 990
- Have at least one filing during 2019–2023
- Have a health-related mission or NTEE classification

## Initial Health Categories

The feasibility analysis will initially examine:

1. Community clinics and outpatient health services
2. Mental-health and crisis-intervention organizations
3. Disease prevention, treatment, and patient-support organizations
4. Disability-related health and support services
5. Public-health and health-education organizations

## Categories Evaluated Separately

- Hospitals and hospital systems
- Nursing facilities
- Medical research organizations

These organizations may have very different financial structures and could distort comparisons with community-based organizations.

## Exclusions

- Organizations outside King County
- Organizations that are not 501(c)(3)
- Form 990-EZ and Form 990-N filers
- Organizations with no usable financial filing
- Insurance companies
- For-profit healthcare providers
- Organizations whose missions are not substantially health-related

## Unit of Analysis

Each row in the primary analytical dataset will represent one organization filing for one tax year.

## Analytical Boundary

The project measures organizational financial capacity and resilience. It does not measure healthcare quality, patient outcomes, or organizational effectiveness.
